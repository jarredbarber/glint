Glint Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 August 2026

Glint is a client-side static wiki. It runs entirely in your browser. There is no Glint server, no user database, and no analytics. Glint stores and transmits no user data outside your own browser and the Google Drive folder you choose to open.

What Glint accesses

  • When you connect Google Drive, Glint requests the drive.file scope. This lets it read and write only the files it creates or that you explicitly open through the Google file picker. It cannot see the rest of your Drive.
  • Drive access is used solely to list, read, and save Markdown pages and comments in the folder you selected.

What Glint stores

  • Your Google OAuth access token is kept in your browser's local storage so you are not prompted on every page load. It is short-lived (about one hour) and never leaves your browser.
  • Glint collects no personal information, sends nothing to any third party, and has no back end to receive your data.

Removing access

You can revoke Glint's access to your Google account at any time from Google Account permissions. Clearing your browser's site data removes any stored token.
